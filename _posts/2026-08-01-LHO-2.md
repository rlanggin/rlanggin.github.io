## A Saturday Tour with Fred Raab

I also tagged along on one of the First Saturday Public Tours, led by Fred Raab, who's been with LIGO since the very beginning. Getting the history from someone who was actually there isn't something I take for granted, so I showed up on a Saturday for this one.

Fred told us about the installation and what's been learned after 20 years of running. My favorite detail: the beam tubes were insulated when they went in, but after years of operating at Livingston, the insulation started failing. Turned out rodents had been chewing through it, and crews had to go down the arms pulling the damaged sections out by hand. An instrument that can measure changes smaller than a fraction of a proton, taken down by rats.

I also spent half the tour listening as a fellow and half as a future tour guide, taking mental notes on which questions people kept asking. Filing those away for whenever I run one of these myself.
