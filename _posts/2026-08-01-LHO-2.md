# Two Months In: Tours, Tutorials, and Pumping Down

It's hard to believe two months have gone by since I first arrived at LIGO Hanford Observatory. I've had the chance to participate in a regional conference, many meetings, go on a public tour of the observatory, and I even licked the concrete encasing the x-arm beam tube.

## First Saturday Tours

I tagged along on tours led by Fred Raab, who has been part of LIGO since the very beginning. Getting hear the history of the detector from someone who was there for it is not something I take for granted, so I was eager to go to work on my Saturday. He told us about the installation history and what we have learned after 20 years of operation. For example, the beam tubes were insulated when they were installed but after years of operating at Livingston small issues began to appear. They found rodents were causing the insulation to fail and had to go along the arms removing it.

I also got to listen to common questions other tour attendees asked,m which I've taken note of for my own public tour in the future.
