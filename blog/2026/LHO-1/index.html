LHO Fellows Program – Weeks 1 & 2

Created in July 05, 2026 by Rachel Langgin

2026   ·   LIGO,   astronomy,   research   ·   lho-fellows

Settling In at LIGO Hanford Observatory

My first two weeks at LHO have already flown by! Between orientation, meeting the other fellows, beginning my research project, exploring the observatory, and enjoying the beautiful Washington scenery, every day has been packed with something new.

Obligatory selfie in front of x-arm at LIGO Hanford Observatory, one of the two 4 km (2.5 mi) vacuum tubes configured in an L-shape that makes the interferometer.

During my first week I spent time getting familiar with the observatory, meeting scientists and engineers, and learning more about the projects I’ll be working on this summer and fall.

Around LIGO & Inside the Laser Vacuum Equipment Area (LVEA)

Exploring the observatory during my first week.
A closer look inside the LVEA.

The more time I spend here, the more I appreciate just how much work goes into keeping the detectors operating at such extraordinary precision.

Every day seemed to introduce another fascinating part of the observatory. It has been exciting to finally see the facilities that I’ve read so much about and begin learning how everything fits together.

Evenings with the Fellows

Picnics, dinners, and spending time together outside of work.

Some of my favorite moments have happened after the workday ends. Whether we’re sharing dinner, having a picnic, or simply relaxing together, it’s been a great opportunity to get to know everyone.

Fourth of July

Celebrating Independence Day with the other fellows.

We wrapped up the holiday weekend with good food, great company, and fireworks. It was the perfect way to end an unforgettable first two weeks.
