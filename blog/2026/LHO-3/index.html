LHO Fellows Program – Four months at Hanford

Created in October 13, 2026 by Rachel Langgin

2026   ·   LIGO   Gravitational-Waves   research   ·   lho-fellows

Four Months In

I’m four months into my six month fellowship. I swear, time is accelerated here. I’ve gotten to explore more of the LVEA and seen more of the Tri-Cities. It has been a very busy time commissioning and preparing for the upcoming observing run.

Me on the roof with the X-arm behind me.

Keep reading for more updates!

Inside the LVEA

Back in the Laser and Vacuum Equipment Area (LVEA), the Horizontal Access Modules (HAMs) are all bolted up and we have pumped down the corner station to a vacuum state. It was a lot quieter (commissioning time!) these past few times going in there since we have been trying to lock the interferometer and get us back to a low-noise observing state.

The overpass, the Y-arm enclosure, HAM7 all closed up, the bat hazard sign, me at the new bigger beam splitter, and me with the H2 beam-tube to be sent to LIGO India.

As we move into Autumn and spooky season, it seems the natural environment also got the memo since there was a bat that almost got loose in the LVEA! It hasn’t been seen in a few weeks, so we think it escaped – but I learned where the emergency net is located for next time.

Back in the Control Room

Commissioning the detector is unpredictable; new tasks and problems that need to be solved arise every day which makes it an exciting time to be around.

The control room when it was quiet on a Friday, and one of my favorite spots on the couch.

Up on the Roof

The roof crew, and the view down the X-arm. Add names, left to right, if you like.

Fall Fest at Goose Ridge Winery

Fall fest, the hay, and me at the vineyard.

The Petting Zoo

The petting zoo, starring sunflowers, a cow, a ram, sheep, and a very serious turkey.

Outside of Work

Since August, my free time has been filled with fun social events! The fellows made it out to a Tri-Cities Americans hockey game, went over to Marc Pirello’s house for some Guitar Hero, and visited the geese and ducks at the Waterfowl Habitat.

The hockey game, Guitar Hero, and the geese, heading to the river and in it.
Dinner back home in Vegas for Labor Day weekend! I'm so happy I got to meet up with a bunch of my friends for the weekend at my favorite Japanese Curry spot.

There’s two months left of my fellowship here and plenty to do! Check back in to read my closing updates soon.
