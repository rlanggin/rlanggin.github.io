Inside the LVEA

Back in the Laser Vacuum and Equipment Area (LVEA), the Horizontal Access Modules (HAMs) are all bolted up and we have pumped down the corner station to a vacuum state. It was a lot quieter (commissioning time!) these past few times going in there since we have been trying to lock the interferometer and get us back to a low-noise observing state.

[Caption: the overpass, the Y-arm enclosure, HAM7 all closed up, the bat hazard sign, me at the new bigger beam splitter, and me with the H2 beam-tube to be sent to LIGO India.]

As we move into Autumn and spooky season, it seems the natural environment also got the memo since there was a bat that almost got loose in the LVEA! It hasn’t been seen in a few weeks, so we think it escaped – but I learned where the emergency net is located for next time.
